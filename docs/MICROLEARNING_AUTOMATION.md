# Microlearning — four ideas, finite feed

Owner request, 22 October 2026. Generate four original Italian cards per local Europe/Rome day, stored permanently in `content/microlearning/editions/YYYY-MM-DD.json`. Update `content/microlearning/manifest.json` atomically in the same commit. Never remove an older edition or silently rewrite IDs of saved cards. No backlog, streak, completion target, pressure, obligatory quiz or infinite/autoloaded historical feed. Archive is an explicit next choice after “Per oggi è tutto”. Saved copies stay in local state and private device backups.

## Editorial direction

A pleasant alternative to a few minutes on Instagram. Discovery and delight matter: the hook should make the reader curious; a concrete example/image should make one idea comprehensible. Each card is complete, not a teaser that hides its answer. Prefer roughly 60–110 words of core reading, at most three short paragraphs, plus a brief hook and takeaway. Do not split an essay into four similar blocks. No academic voice, “the author says” commentary, repetitive disclaimers or generic motivational aphorisms. Put source context, editorial additions and necessary qualifications in `basis`; keep any scientifically essential uncertainty in the body as well.

Vary the four: nature/ecology/biology, philosophy or scientific worldviews, engineering/computing, human experience or judgement. These are directions, not quotas: avoid forcing technical usefulness on nature or literature. Draw from the public reading candidates in `docs/EDITORIAL_AUTOMATION.md` and `content/editorial/policy.json`, plus sources found through actual research. Add Stephen Wolfram, *What Ultimately Is There?... And Other Foundational Questions* (verified title and primary essays); Francesca Mastrogiacomi, *Empathy and Artificial Intelligence in Action: A Design Thinking Framework for Human-Centred Innovation* (publisher front matter and actually accessible sections). An index or marketing description does not count as reading a book. Independently authored examples must not be attributed to an author. Distinguish hypotheses, interpretations, fiction and established results without turning a card into disclaimers. Include nature regularly beyond its use for BlueRev.

Deepstash is a product reference for one idea per card, visual hierarchy, compact reading, source and personal collections. Do not copy its cards, proprietary assets or marketing claims. Use autonomous explanations and primary sources actually consulted, within copyright limits. Do not reconstruct copyrighted pages or imitate living authors' distinctive styles.

## Format and visuals

Read `src/microlearning.js` and `src/microlearning-view.js`. Contract: schema 1, date, exactly four cards with globally unique stable lowercase IDs, topic, title, hook, paragraphs, takeaway, basis, HTTPS sources, tone and visual. Allowed tones: sage, clay, plum, blue. Visual kinds: automaton (only actual Rule 30, never attach it to unrelated data), flow (current renderer is a leaf/water schematic, use only for that subject), contrast (one to three short labels), question (one evocative question). Match visuals to meaning. Vary layouts through contrast/question, not random pictograms or irrelevant scientific diagrams. These templates are an initial set, not permission to falsely illustrate a subject. No raw HTML/SVG/JS from generated content. Do not add software or image-generation costs from this content job.

Read at least 30 previous editions and compare concepts, not just titles. Avoid repeating the same idea or merely reducing the daily editorial to four cards. A later related idea should add a concrete new connection. A rerun must be a no-op when the target date is already present, unless the owner specifically requests a correction.
